Policies & Service Terms.

Please review these important conditions before proceeding with a request.

Privacy Policy

PrimePath respects the privacy of people who contact us. Information shared in an enquiry should be used only for understanding and responding to that request and any further service the customer chooses to pursue.

When you use the enquiry form, the details you enter are placed into a WhatsApp message. The message is not sent until you review and choose to send it through WhatsApp.

Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, banking credentials or sensitive identity documents in an initial enquiry. If further documentation is required for an agreed service, an appropriate handling method should be discussed first.

Terms of Service

PrimePath Documentation Services is an independent private service provider. We are not a government agency, embassy, court, issuing authority or official representative of any institution.

Assistance may include general information, administrative preparation, coordination and follow-up, depending on the request and confirmed capacity.

Official decisions, approvals, fees, processing times and document issuance remain the responsibility of the relevant authorities or service providers. No particular outcome is guaranteed.

The scope, fees and limitations of any agreed service should be confirmed before work begins.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Any applicable PrimePath service fee, official charge or third-party cost should be identified and explained before a customer confirms a service.

Customers who wish to cancel should raise the request as soon as possible. Any refund assessment will take account of work already completed, costs already incurred and non-refundable official or third-party charges.

Where a service has not started, the applicable refund position should be discussed with the customer. Customers should retain confirmation of agreed payment, cancellation or refund arrangements.

Service Delivery Policy

Communication, administrative assistance and agreed updates are handled through the communication method confirmed with the customer.

Expected completion timelines, where they can reasonably be provided, depend on the nature of the request and any relevant external authority or provider.

PrimePath cannot control delays caused by government agencies, institutions, courier services, technical systems or other third parties.

Where physical documents or courier arrangements are involved, the applicable arrangements, costs and responsibilities should be agreed before proceeding.
