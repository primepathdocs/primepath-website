Need Clarification?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about how PrimePath operates.

Is PrimePath a government agency?

No. PrimePath Documentation Services is an independent private service provider. We are not a government agency, embassy, immigration authority or official government portal.

How much do your services cost?

Our charges are not fixed across all services. Costs depend on the specific request, applicable official fees, third-party provider charges and any agreed PrimePath administrative or service fee. A breakdown is provided before you decide whether to proceed.

Can I request assistance from outside Nigeria?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from individuals and businesses in Nigeria and internationally. Service availability depends on the documentation process and relevant jurisdiction.

Does PrimePath issue passports, NIN or government certificates?

No. Official documents are issued by the relevant authorised government agencies or institutions. Our role is to provide administrative assistance and coordinate eligible processes.

Can PrimePath guarantee approval?

No. Decisions concerning approvals, registrations, applications and official document issuance remain with the relevant authorities. We cannot guarantee an outcome or processing time controlled by a third party.

How do I get started?

Begin by making an enquiry about the service you need. We will assess your request, explain the applicable requirements and communicate the relevant costs.

Is my personal information protected?

We treat customer information with care and request only information reasonably necessary for an agreed service. Customers should never share passwords or one-time verification codes with us.
